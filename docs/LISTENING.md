## What to listen for

- Does the melody go somewhere, or wander?
- Does the section change feel earned (a lift) or arbitrary?
- Is anything buried — a bass you can feel but not hear, a lead that fights the chords?
- At 20 BPM and at 500 BPM: does the same song still make sense?
